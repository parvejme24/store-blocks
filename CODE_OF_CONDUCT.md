# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

StoreBlocks adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct. Everyone participating in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, and events) is expected to follow it.

We want StoreBlocks to be a welcoming place for contributors of every background and experience level, including first-time open-source contributors.

## In Short

**Do:** be respectful and patient, give constructive feedback, accept feedback gracefully, assume good intent, and help newcomers.

**Do not:** harass, insult, or demean anyone; publish others' private information; or engage in behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers at **conduct@storeblocks.dev**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from project spaces.
